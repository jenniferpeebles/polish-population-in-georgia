# Reporter brief

Source: **2020-2024 ACS five-year estimates**. These measures overlap and must not be added.

## Georgia statewide estimates

- **People reporting Polish ancestry: 104,214** (90% MOE: +/-3,439; interval: 100,775-107,653). This passes the reliability screen.
- **Foreign-born residents born in Poland: 3,096** (90% MOE: +/-592; interval: 2,504-3,688). This passes the reliability screen.
- **People age 5+ who speak Polish at home: 3,361** (90% MOE: +/-662; interval: 2,699-4,023). This passes the reliability screen.

## Language note

The Polish-at-home total is 3,361. Of that total, 797 are estimated to speak English less than very well. The pipeline uses the published total rather than adding it to its components.

## Do not overstate

Ancestry, birthplace and language are different concepts. Language covers people age 5+. Birthplace excludes U.S.-born descendants. Keep each 90% MOE with its estimate.
